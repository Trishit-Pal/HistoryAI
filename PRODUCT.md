# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A visitor walking the history of artificial intelligence at night, stopping at one sourced exhibit. They may be new to the field or already reading papers. They can stop at the public story, or keep going into sources and notes.

## Product Purpose

Hall of Eras is a walkable museum of technical AI history and its documented context. Success is a visitor who can move a year rail, enter a hall, open one exhibit, see how sure the museum is, and follow a source URL. The corridor is the experience. The exhibit page is where sources, the change in the field, and the museum's own revisions are read. Anyone can suggest a correction. There is no account.

## Brand Personality

A set of drawings: chalk linework on blueprint blue, one redline for where you are, ink on a pale print for reading. Quiet except for the year rail. Voice is plain and specific. It names years, papers, and halls. It does not sell the field.

## Anti-references

Cream or parchment pages. Acid green or vermilion on near-black. Newspaper columns. Sci-fi robots, glass cards, and numbered marketing eyebrows (01 / 02 / 03) on every section.

## Design Principles

- The year rail is the only ornamental motion.
- A claim is never shown without a confidence word and a source path.
- The hall name lives in the room, so a one-pedestal hall is still a place.
- Chalk draws the hall; redline marks only where you are. On the pale print, ink does the reading.
- A hall is a sheet. Sealed halls are not yet drawn, and the AI winters are drawn with fainter lines.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard can walk halls that are not sealed. Focus rings are visible. Reduced motion keeps the corridor and snaps the camera. Flatland is the path when WebGL is missing. Body text on the plate meets ordinary contrast. Redline is never the only cue: the current hall also has a heavier underline and each confidence word has its own shape.
