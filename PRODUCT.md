# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A visitor walking the history of artificial intelligence at night, stopping at one sourced exhibit. They may be new to the field or already reading papers. They can stop at the public story, or keep going into sources and notes.

## Product Purpose

Hall of Eras is a walkable museum of technical AI history and its documented context. Success is a visitor who can move a year rail, enter a hall, read one exhibit, see how sure the museum is, and follow a source URL. The corridor is the experience. The plate is a reading tool inside it.

## Brand Personality

Stone, brass, ink. Quiet except for the year rail. Voice is plain and specific. It names years, papers, and halls. It does not sell the field.

## Anti-references

Cream or parchment pages. Acid green or vermilion on near-black. Newspaper columns. Sci-fi robots, glass cards, and numbered marketing eyebrows (01 / 02 / 03) on every section.

## Design Principles

- The year rail is the only ornamental motion.
- A claim is never shown without a confidence word and a source path.
- The hall name lives in the room, so a one-pedestal hall is still a place.
- Brass is metal on stone. On the zinc plate, ink does the reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard can walk halls that are not sealed. Focus rings are visible. Reduced motion keeps the corridor and snaps the camera. Flatland is the path when WebGL is missing. Body text on the plate meets ordinary contrast. Brass is not used as small text on zinc.
